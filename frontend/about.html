Our methodology

Follow the evidence.

How we turn a video's claims into a report you can inspect.

How a video is checked

  1. Read the transcript

    We retrieve the transcript from YouTube captions. Everything starts with what the video actually says.

  2. Identify the claims

    Claude identifies check-worthy claims and rewrites each one to stand alone, preserving its meaning and approximate speaker attribution.

  3. Find the evidence

    For each claim, we run targeted web searches through Brave Search plus Google's database of professional fact-checks. We rank sources by reliability.

  4. Judge and verify

    Claude judges the claim against that evidence and must quote the exact passage it relies on. We verify every quote against the source text and discard citations that don't match. Claims without verifiable support are marked Unverified rather than guessed.

Verdict scale

Accuracy score

The score is a confidence-weighted average over checkable claims: True 95, Mostly true 75, Mixed 50, Mostly false 25, False 5. Unverified claims and opinions are excluded.

A report with no checkable claims has no accuracy score. The score describes the checked claims, not the creator's overall credibility.

Source reliability

  • Fact-checkerProfessional fact-checking organizations, such as Full Fact and PolitiFact.
  • High reliabilityPrimary research, government agencies, universities, and established reporting.
  • General sourceGeneral publications and reference websites.
  • User-generatedForums, social posts, and other user contributions.
  • UnknownSources we cannot confidently place in another tier.

Limitations

Captions can be wrong. Auto-captions may mishear names and numbers, and speaker attribution is approximate. Search results can miss recent or paywalled information.

AI can make mistakes. Read the cited sources before relying on a verdict, especially for anything important.

Privacy

We store the video ID, results, and a truncated IP address for rate limiting. No accounts, no cookies. Your theme preference stays in your browser.

Verdicts are produced by AI from public web sources and can be wrong. Check the cited sources for anything important.
